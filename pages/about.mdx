# About

**Beplus Site Assistant** is a WordPress plugin that adds an AI chat assistant to
your site, answering visitor questions from your own content rather than from
general knowledge.

## What makes it different

Most chat widgets answer from a generic model, which means they invent prices,
policies and opening hours with total confidence. This one reads your actual
pages first, answers from what it finds, and shows the visitor the sources it
used.

The second half of the idea is on the admin side. Every conversation is kept, and
one press turns them into a plain answer to a question most sites never manage to
answer: **what should we put on the website next?** The subjects people ask about
are your content plan, and the ones your site cannot answer are your missing
pages.

## Who it is for

- **Small businesses** with a handful of pages who want support questions
  answered without hiring anyone
- **Agencies** running several client sites — the FAQ list and settings can be
  exported and reused, and the Embed Script screen puts one assistant on both
  WordPress and non-WordPress sites
- **Anyone whose site is mostly static** — the content is already there, it just
  needs to be answerable

## What it is not

- **Not a replacement for a human** on anything that matters. The assistant
  answers factual questions from your site; it escalates by email, not by taking
  decisions.
- **Not a live chat product** with operators on the other end. It answers by
  itself around the clock.
- **Not free to run** if you use a paid AI provider — every question is a small
  call to that provider. The
  **[Security & Limits](/security)** tab exists to cap that cost.

## Privacy

- **Your API key** is stored on your server and is **never sent to a browser**.
  The settings screen can replace it but cannot display it back.
- **Your content** goes to whichever AI provider you configure, and to no one
  else. In Custom endpoint mode, that is a provider you chose yourself.
- **Conversation data** is stored in **your own database**. Nothing is deleted
  automatically, and nothing is sent anywhere except to your chosen AI provider
  and, if you turn it on, to your notification email.
- **Visitor names and emails** are only collected if you switch on the lead form.
  They are stored with the conversation and emailed to the address you set.
