# Introduction

**Beplus Site Assistant** puts an AI chat assistant on your website. Visitors ask
questions in their own words and get answers taken from your actual pages — not
from a generic model guessing at what your business probably does.

It is not a scripted chatbot. There is no decision tree to maintain, no list of
keywords to keep up to date. You point it at your site, and it reads what is
already there.

## What it knows

- Your **pages, posts and products** — the text, not just the titles
- Your **custom post types**, menus and their links
- Your **forms**, search, comments and login pages
- Whether **WooCommerce** is running, and its cart and checkout
- Any **extra sources** you add — a web page, or a Markdown file you upload
- **Company rules you write yourself** — opening hours, hotlines, what it may and
  may not discuss

## What visitors see

A floating assistant in the corner of every page, opening into a normal chat
window. Answers arrive with the **sources** they came from, so a visitor can
check for themselves rather than taking the answer on trust.

## What you get on the admin side

| Screen | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings** | Connect the AI, choose what to index, style the widget, collect leads, set limits |
| **Conversations** | Every conversation your visitors have had, with transcripts and email delivery |
| **SEO Insights** | What people actually ask about — and what your site has no page for |
| **Embed Script** | Put the same assistant on a website that does not run WordPress |

## The things people ask first

**Does it send my content to an AI provider?**

Yes — that is what makes it answer in natural language. You choose the provider.
In **Custom endpoint** mode you paste the address of whichever provider you want,
so you decide where your content goes. The plugin itself never sends anything
anywhere else.

**Can it leak my API key or my code?**

No. The key is stored on your server and is never sent to the browser — the
settings screen can replace it but cannot show it back to you. The assistant is
also instructed to describe features rather than reveal source code or
credentials, and it refuses those requests outright.

**Where is the conversation data kept?**

In your own database. Nothing is deleted automatically, and deleting a
conversation is something you do deliberately.
