# Storage

Conversations are kept **forever** by default. This tab tells you what that is
costing you in disk space, and gives you the one button that deletes things.

![Conversations, Storage tab](/img/08-conversations-storage.png)

## Why nothing is deleted automatically

Because the conversations are the valuable part. The SEO report, the gap
analysis, the record of what your visitors asked — all of it comes from these
records. A plugin that quietly deletes them after thirty days would be throwing
away the most useful data on the site.

So the rule is simple: **nothing is ever deleted unless you delete it.**

## How much space it takes

Conversations are text, and text is small. A conversation with a dozen exchanges
is a few kilobytes — less than a single product photo.

For scale: hundreds of thousands of conversations would still be measured in
hundreds of megabytes, not gigabytes. On any normal site this is not a figure
worth worrying about.

The numbers on this tab are read live from the database, so they are the real
figures rather than an estimate.

> 💡 **What is actually worth watching is your server's free disk**, not the size
> of this table. A chat log has never filled a disk; a forgotten backup has. The
> plugin shows the free space alongside its own size so the proportion is
> obvious.

## Manual cleanup

The **Delete conversations older than** control removes everything before a date
you choose.

This is the right tool when:

- You are running a campaign that is over, and the older conversations are noise
- You need to reclaim space on a very tight hosting plan
- You are handing the site to someone else and want a clean slate

> ⚠️ **This is one-way, and it is not a backup.**
> Deleting removes the whole exchange — including what the assistant remembered
> about those visitors. The conversation record is your only copy of what was
> said, so **export first** if there is any chance you will want the history.
> See **[Conversations](/conversations)** for the Export button.

After a delete, the SEO report no longer counts those conversations. The topic
tally itself is kept, so the analysis you already paid for is not thrown away —
but the underlying conversations behind it are gone.

## Exporting before you clean up

A sensible routine if you do want to trim:

1. **Export** the whole range to CSV or JSONL —
   **[Conversations](/conversations)** → **Export**
2. Keep the file somewhere safe — a backup disk, cloud storage, wherever your
   other backups go
3. **Only then** delete from this tab

The exported file is a complete record: one row per conversation, with the full
transcript inside it, and it opens in any spreadsheet.
