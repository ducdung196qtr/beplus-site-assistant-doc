# Knowledge Base

This is where you decide **what the assistant is allowed to know**. Everything on
this tab feeds the answers.

![Settings, Knowledge Base tab](/img/02-settings-knowledge-v2.png)

## Scanning your site

Press **Re-scan site** to read your content and build the searchable index. The
first scan is the slow one; later scans only pick up what changed.

The plugin also re-scans **once a day by itself**, so new posts and edited prices
are picked up without you thinking about it.

**Scan mode** controls how that work is done:

| Mode | What it does | Choose it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Auto (batched)** | Works through the site in small batches, holding only a little in memory at a time | Larger sites, or a modest hosting plan |
| **Full (single pass)** | Reads everything in one go | Small sites — slightly faster |

If a scan ever fails part-way on a big site, switching to **Auto** is the usual
fix.

### What gets indexed

Two permissions, and they are independent:

- **Site Content** — posts, pages and custom post types. This covers most sites
  completely.
- **E-commerce (WooCommerce)** — product prices, SKUs, variations, stock status
  and descriptions. Off by default, and worth turning on only if you actually
  sell through the site.

The **Site Status** panel shows what was found — counts per group, and the titles
it picked up — so you can confirm the right things were read rather than assuming.

## Custom Instructions & Business Rules

A free-text box for the things that are true about your business but are not
written on any page.

Put here:

- Opening hours and holidays
- Hotline numbers and support email
- Delivery areas and timeframes
- Return policy in one sentence
- Anything the assistant should **never** discuss

Instructions here take priority over general knowledge, so this is the right
place for corrections like "we do not ship outside the EU" when a page is less
clear about it.

> 💡 **Short and factual beats long and polite.**
> "Open Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00 CET. Closed public holidays." works better than a
> paragraph explaining that the team is always happy to help during business
> hours.

## External Docs & Web Sources

Content that lives outside WordPress — a help centre, a PDF-turned-page,
documentation on another domain.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **+ Add Web URL / Doc** | Fetches a page and stores its text. If the page has sub-pages you can choose to follow them too. |
| **+ Upload Markdown (.md)** | Upload a `.md` file directly — useful for product manuals and internal notes. |
| **🔄 Re-sync All Docs** | Re-fetches every URL source, for when a page was edited at the source. |
| **🗑️ Clear All External Docs** | Removes all external sources and the chunks indexed from them. Nothing else is touched. |

Each source can be refreshed or removed on its own, so one outdated page does not
force you to rebuild everything.

## Keeping it accurate

The index is a copy of your content, not a live look-up. Two habits keep it
honest:

1. **After a big content change**, press **Re-scan site** rather than waiting for
   the nightly run.
2. **When an answer is wrong**, check whether the page it came from is wrong or
   missing — the **[SEO Insights](/seo-insights)** gap report is built for
   exactly that question.
